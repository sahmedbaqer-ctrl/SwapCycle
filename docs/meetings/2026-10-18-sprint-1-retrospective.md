# Sprint 1 – Sprint Retrospective

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 18 October 2026 |
| **Format** | Written retrospective at the end of Sprint 1, put together by the team from the sprint's meeting records |

## What went well
- The project topic was agreed in the first meeting.
- Roles and feature ownership were assigned early.
- The GitHub repository and Jira board were set up and shared with everyone.

## What didn't go well
- Attendance was incomplete in two meetings (one absence, one late arrival).
- User stories were written before being entered into Jira, which delayed board updates.

## Agreed improvements for Sprint 2
- Enter work in Jira as it is done, not afterwards.
- Assign an owner to every feature before the next sprint.
- Share the meeting agenda in advance so absent members can send updates.
