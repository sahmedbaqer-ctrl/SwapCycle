## Sprint Retrospective

**What went well**
- Tasks were split into pairs early, so everyone knew their part.
- Regular check-ups kept track of each task's progress.
- The wireframes and architectural comparison were finished on time.

**What didn't go well**
- The class and sequence diagrams were left until the final day.
- One member was late to the last three meetings.
- Most check-ups were bunched into the last four days.

**Actions for next sprint**
- Set an internal deadline for diagrams a few days before submission.
- Hold at least two stand-ups every week.
- Start meetings on time and share updates in the group chat if running late.
